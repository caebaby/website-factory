## Who They Serve (ICP)

**Describe their ideal client:**
Business owners/executives worth roughly $40-50M, with complex estate-planning needs.

> "Really high net worth clients who really and truly — it's not that they've done nothing, but they've done very little [planning]. They are scared to death about having somebody work on their stuff, and they're not willing to open up their financial lives until they're referred to somebody, referred from somebody they trust to somebody that person trusts." — Jesse Holman

**Counter-intuitive fact:** despite the wealth level, they typically have done very little formal planning — the opposite of what you'd expect.

**Access:** referral-gated only — "their mouths are shut and their lives are shut until they get introduced" by someone they already trust. Not searchable, not ad-reachable in the traditional sense.

**Insider language:**
> "There's a completely different language for rich people. The second you don't speak that language, or you speak it with a heavy dialect, they know you don't understand them."

Speaking their language correctly causes them to actively refer ("this dude speaks our language, you got to go work with him").

**Their #1 problem/pain:** They've done very little planning despite massive wealth, and they're scared to let anyone work on their stuff. They won't open up until referred by someone they trust.

**What makes them nervous:** Being approached by someone who doesn't speak their language — they instantly know you don't understand them.
