## Business
- **Name:** Verdant Studio
- **What it is:** Brand design studio exclusively for sustainable/climate-conscious startups —
  identity systems, packaging, and conversion-focused web presence.
- **Offer:** "Brand Assessment" (free diagnostic) → full brand identity engagement.
- **Positioning seed:** most sustainable brands blend into a sea of sage-green sameness; real
  sustainability deserves a brand that *proves* it instead of costuming it.

## ICP + pains
- **Who:** founders of sustainable CPG/DTC startups (seed to Series A) whose product ethics are
  genuine but whose brand looks like a template — and they know it.
- **Pains (seed hypotheses for research):**
  - "We look like every other eco brand" — leaf logos, kraft paper, sage green.
  - Conscious consumers *want* to trust them but the brand reads unproven/DTC-cheap.
  - Premium pricing is impossible when the brand looks generic.
  - Agencies either "get" sustainability or "get" conversion — never both.
  - Greenwashing anxiety: terrified of looking like fakers because the real fakers took the
    visual language first.
- **Insider language seeds:** greenwashing, B-Corp, LCA, "kraft-paper trap," conscious consumer,
  climate claims, CPG shelf test.

## Brand inputs
- **Palette:** deep forest green ground (#1D3B2A register), warm cream (#FAF3E3), single gold
  accent (#C9A24B). Retint per PALETTES.md math.
- **Fonts:** display = DM Serif Display (or register-equivalent serif display); body =
  Source Sans 3 (or equivalent humanist sans with true 300/600 range).
- **Visual world:** living-material warmth — real texture, botanical shadow, printed-object
  photography; NEVER stock leaves/globes/hands-holding-seedlings.
- **Signature-motif seed:** the "transformation reveal" — generic template state vs. Verdant
  state of the same brand, one interactive moment (Mahdin's hero comparison card is the seed).
