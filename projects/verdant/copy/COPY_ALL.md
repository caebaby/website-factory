### PAIN — The Visual Credibility Crisis

**Eyebrow:** Why Generic Eco-Brands Fail

**H2:** The kraft-paper trap is real. And it's costing you revenue.

**Pain points (in order):**

**1. "We look like every other eco brand"**
Your product is genuinely sustainable. Your mission is real. But your brand? Sage green, leaf logo, kraft paper packaging—it's the same template 50 other brands shipped. Conscious consumers see generic before they see genuine.

**2. "Conscious consumers want to trust us, but our brand reads unproven"**
78% of conscious consumers say brand design influences their trust in climate claims. The gap between "I want to support you" and "I believe you" is visual. Your current brand doesn't close it.

**3. "Premium pricing is impossible when the brand looks generic"**
You can't charge premium prices for premium ethics if the design says off-the-shelf. Retail partners don't take you seriously. Direct customers leave you in the cart. The cost of looking cheap is giving away margin.

**4. "Agencies either get sustainability or get conversion—never both"**
Most design studios treat sustainability as an aesthetic. Most conversion agencies cut corners on craft to hit timelines. You need both: design that respects your climate DNA and transforms browsers into buyers on day one. That combination is rare.
